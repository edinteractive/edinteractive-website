How the audit works

Your data stays yours

  • You approve read-only access through Zoho's own sign-in. We never see your password.
  • Nothing in your CRM, Books or Survey is changed, and learner data is never shared.
  • Access is removed automatically after 14 days, or sooner if you ask.

How the numbers are estimated

  • Values use your own renewal price, cohort value, win rate and invoice history.
  • Each estimate is checked by a person before the report is sent.
  • They are estimates, not guarantees. We track the real results with you after each fix.
